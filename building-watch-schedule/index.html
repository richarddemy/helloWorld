    How publishing works

    • Submitted hours never reach a calendar. Only the watch does.
    • A watch stays a draft, seen only by admins, until you publish it.
    • Taking someone off a published watch removes their calendar event right away.
    • To let everyone see the full watch on their own calendar, share this calendar from Google Calendar's settings.
